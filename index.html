PRIVACY

Your Privacy Matters

Respect for privacy and confidentiality is an important principle of Dr. MAFT's healthcare advisory and counselling services.

Information You Share

Please provide only the information necessary to handle your enquiry or consultation request. Avoid including sensitive medical details in general email subject lines or messages unless an appropriate channel has been agreed upon.

Third-Party Booking Services

The booking buttons open Calendly, an external scheduling service. Information entered on its booking pages may be processed by Calendly under its own privacy policy and terms. Please review those terms before submitting personal information.

Email communications may pass through third-party email providers. Do not assume that information submitted through email, Calendly or this website is stored exclusively by Dr. MAFT or is inaccessible to service providers.

Payments and Medical Documents

Do not send payment card numbers, security codes, passwords or other payment credentials by email. Use only a verified payment process when one has been provided. Before sharing medical reports or other sensitive documents, confirm the appropriate channel.

Booking Statistics

Public statistics are intended to show aggregate booking counts only. Country-wise reports should be accessible only through an authenticated private dashboard. The actual collection, storage, access and retention arrangements must be configured and verified before live statistics are enabled.

Data Handling

Personal information should be accessed and used only for legitimate purposes associated with the enquiry, booking or requested service, subject to applicable legal obligations. Information may also be processed by service providers involved in scheduling, communications or payments.

This page does not by itself guarantee encryption, exclusive access, a particular storage arrangement or compliance with every country's privacy laws.

Healthcare Disclaimer

Online medical guidance does not replace an appropriate clinical examination, diagnosis or treatment by a healthcare professional licensed to provide the relevant care in your jurisdiction.
