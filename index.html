ABOUT

Professional Healthcare Guidance

Dr. MAFT provides independent international medical advisory, counselling and healthcare navigation services designed to help individuals better understand their healthcare needs.

With experience as a general physician and rehabilitation officer, the service also encompasses guidance related to psychiatric, substance-use and rehabilitation contexts.

The goal is to provide clear, professional and understandable healthcare guidance so that individuals can make informed decisions about their next steps.

PRIVACY

Your Privacy Matters

Patient privacy and confidentiality are important principles of this service.

Information submitted for consultation purposes is intended for the provision of professional healthcare guidance. Access and handling should be limited to authorized purposes.

Patients should avoid sharing unnecessary sensitive personal information. Any medical information provided should be limited to what is relevant to the consultation.

Medical information should not be considered a substitute for examination, diagnosis or treatment by a licensed healthcare professional in your jurisdiction.
